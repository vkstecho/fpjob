How it works

  1. Admin adds your mobile to the member list (Excel upload or manual).
  2. You log in with OTP — no passwords to remember.
  3. Complete your profile, explore members, post or apply for jobs, build your resume.
  4. Install the app on your phone for a home-screen shortcut and faster access.
