Delete Your Account

FPJOB · LAST UPDATED: 8 MAY 2026

This page explains how to permanently delete your FPJob account and all associated personal data, in compliance with the Google Play Store account deletion policy and Indian data protection requirements.

⚠️ WARNING: Account deletion is permanent and irreversible. Once deleted:
  • Your profile will be removed from the member directory
  • All job posts you created will be removed
  • Your resume file will be deleted from our servers
  • You will need to register again from scratch if you wish to return
  • Your phone number can be re-used to register a new account

Method 1: Email Request (Recommended)

Send us an email from the email address registered on your FPJob account, or from any address — we'll verify your identity using your phone number.

Email to: fpjob.vkstech@gmail.com

Subject: FPJob Account Deletion Request

Include in email:

  • Your registered phone number (+91-XXXXXXXXXX)
  • Your registered name
  • Reason for deletion (optional)
  • Statement: "I understand this action is permanent."

📧 Send Deletion Email

What May Be Retained

For legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes, the following may be retained anonymously (without your name/phone) for up to 90 days:

  • Aggregate usage statistics (e.g., total members count)
  • Anonymized error logs (no personal identifiers)
  • Audit trail of admin actions (with your details redacted)

This retained data cannot be linked back to you after deletion.

Confirmation

Once we process your request, you will receive a confirmation:

  • Via email — if you used Method 1
  • Via WhatsApp — if you used Method 2

If you do not receive confirmation within 7 working days, please follow up by emailing fpjob.vkstech@gmail.com.

Pre-Loaded Contacts (Important)

FPJob includes approximately 2,500 pre-loaded industry contacts collected from public WhatsApp packaging industry groups. If you are listed in this directory but never registered, you can still request removal. Use the same email/WhatsApp method above with the subject:

Remove Pre-loaded Contact Listing

Provide your name and phone number. We will remove you from the contact directory within 7 days.

Cancel a Pending Deletion

If you change your mind within 7 days of submitting your deletion request, contact us immediately and we may be able to cancel the deletion. After 7 days, deletion is permanent and cannot be reversed.
